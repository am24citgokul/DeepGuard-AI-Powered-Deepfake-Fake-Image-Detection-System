This third-party model claims 98.4% accuracy but resizes all input images to 32x32 pixels before classification (see app.py line with image.resize((32,32))), and its dataset consists of 32x32 thumbnail images rather than real photographs. This causes catastrophic failure on real-world images, as verified by testing our own photos through it. Kept as a case study illustrating why benchmark accuracy alone does not guarantee real-world reliability.
