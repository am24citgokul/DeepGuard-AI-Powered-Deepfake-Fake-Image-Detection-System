Understanding Grad-CAM neural attention and Error Level Analysis (ELA)

Grad-CAM (Gradient-Weighted Class Activation Mapping)

Highlights the 2D spatial regions inside the convolutional backbone that contributed most heavily to the classification verdict. Red/warm regions denote high activations; blue/cool regions denote low attention.

• Crucial standard: Grad-CAM highlights attention foci, but does not standalone "prove" deepfakes. It serves as an audit signal for forensic analysts.

Error Level Analysis (ELA)

Analyzes quantization delta variations across 8x8 DCT grid matrices by intentionally recompressing the input at 90% quality and scaling the difference matrix by 15x.

• Crucial standard: ELA alone does not prove an image is synthetic; it visualizes compression inconsistencies that commonly arise in multi-layer splicing or face-swaps.
