DEEPGUARD X

AI Identity Authenticity Screening System

DeepGuard X addresses identity spoofing and multimedia fraud by combining single-modality neural feature extraction with a multi-branch consensus engine. Rather than relying on a single fallible classification score, the system examines spatial features, compression artifacts, temporal sequence coherence, and acoustic traits to arrive at an explainable authenticity rating.

Image Modality

EfficientNet-B0 trained on FaceForensics++ C23 across 6 classes with Grad-CAM neural attention and Error Level Analysis (ELA).

Video Modality

CNN-GRU sequence recurrence network extracting inter-frame temporal cues, supported by a multi-frame aggregation fallback provider.

Audio Modality

Acoustic feature analysis measuring spectral flatness, zero-crossing rates, and high-order MFCC variance to detect synthetic vocoder artifacts.

Benchmark Dataset

Trained and evaluated on the standardized FaceForensics++ (C23 light compression) dataset, comprising Original videos alongside 5 representative deepfake manipulation methods: FaceSwap, Deepfakes, Face2Face, NeuralTextures, and FaceShifter.

Academic Disclosure

DeepGuard X is an academic decision-support screening tool. Outputs are formulated as forensic likelihood scores (AUTHENTIC, REVIEW REQUIRED, HIGH RISK) rather than definitive legal proof, fostering accountable human-in-the-loop verification.
